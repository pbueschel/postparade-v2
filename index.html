Trainers ⇄ racing offices

Tracks post their condition books.
Trainers request entries. People decide.

A rebuild of the PostParade prototype around one principle: the software handles the paperwork and reads the conditions. It does not rate horses, forecast races, or recommend anything. Every submission is reviewed by a person at the racing office, and every step is timestamped on all three ends — trainer, track, and system record.

The loop

Four steps, no black box in the middle.

1 · Track

Publish the book

The racing office posts its condition book: every race, its purse, surface, distance, conditions as written, and how many spots it holds.

2 · Trainer

Read it and submit

Select a track, sort the card chronologically, filter by entry close, race date, surface, type, or purse. Name a jockey, flag AE or MTO, submit.

3 · Checker

Check the conditions

Sex, age, maiden and non-winners ladders, claiming and starter clauses, state-bred restrictions, breed registry, vet's list. Conflicts are flagged, never ruled on.

4 · Track

Accept or decline

The office accepts, or declines with a standard reason plus a comment for the one-off cases. The trainer sees the reason, the comment, and the timestamp.

Both sides, same records

Trainer workspace

Kinnon LaRose Racing

  • Dashboard — the barn in three actionable states: active, placement requested, entered. Filterable. No performance figures.
  • My horses — the whole roster; add, remove, pursue races.
  • See condition books — pick a track, read the book, filter it, submit. “See all conditions” shows the raw text when you read a clause differently than the checker.
  • Entry windows — deadlines and watched races, on a page you visit on purpose.
  • Submissions — every request you have sent and what came back.
  • Messages — a stored thread with each racing office.
Track workspace

Ellis Park · Saratoga · Evangeline Downs · Delta Downs

  • Racing office — what is waiting, and which races sit under their minimum field.
  • Our condition book — the book as trainers see it, with spots and pending requests per race.
  • Entry requests — accept, or decline with a reason and a comment on the record.
  • Horses & history — who is entered on each card, plus the standing record of every horse that has come through: new here, or N starts here.
  • Overnight sheets — drafted automatically from accepted entries.

What changed from v1

The first build put a recommendation engine at the centre. This one takes it out and puts the condition book there instead.

v1v2Why
Fit scores, acceptance likelihood, draw-in probability A condition checker that flags conflicts Eligibility is a rule question. Outcome is not our call.
Recommendations screen See condition books → select track → race list Trainers read the book. The app gets them to it faster.
“Spot alerts” and a dashboard block of closing entries One Entry windows page The capability stays; it stops being pushed at you.
Suggested ships, win rates, speed and class figures Records and past performances, as facts Nothing statistical about a future or past result.
Horses tab deep-linked to one horse A real roster for trainers; entries and history for tracks It was a bug, and the tab had no defined contents.
Requests inbox A submissions log, trainer → track Submissions are the record that needs storing.
No messaging Stored threads between office and barn Most placement questions are a conversation.
Hover to act on a horse row The underlined horse name is the link, always Hover did not fire on horses already placed.
Thin horse pages Equibase-shaped profiles Color, sex, state bred, year foaled, sire and dam, connections of last start.

Also in this build

A jockey is named at submission

Required, from a rider list with agents, so the office can catch double-bookings before the entry box closes.

“Submit anyway” when a conflict is flagged

The checker can be wrong, and clauses are ambiguous. The trainer may always override; the flags are snapshotted onto the record so the office reviews what was shown.

AE / MTO designations

Also-eligible and main-track-only requests carry through to the overnight sheet.

Overnights and extras notify

When a card's overnight posts or a race is added after the book, it shows up in the bell and on Entry windows.

Spots are public to both sides

Spots filled, openings left, also-eligible places used, requests awaiting review — one number, same on both ends.

Timestamps on three ends

Submitted, decided, by whom. The trainer view, the office view, and the system activity log read the same append-only rows.

Still open

Equipment at entry — whether the track requires equipment declared at submission is unconfirmed, so the field is present and advisory, never a blocker. Equibase rating — whether it belongs on a horse profile is an open question. Planned, not built: track-branded PDF condition books and overnights, and a question box on the horse profile answered from the record.

About the data

Real where it is cited: trainer, horses, sires and dams, owners, and the results referenced in each profile. Illustrative where it must be: the condition books, race cards, spot counts, messages, and the demo calendar, which rolls forward off the clock so the books are always open. Nothing in here is a rating or a projection, and no number claims to be one. This is a concept prototype, not a system of record.
